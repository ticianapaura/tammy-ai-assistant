# ChatHeader

The top bar of the chat window: the assistant's name with its mark, and the minimize and close buttons.

- Provide `name` from the merchant's brand config; the mark sits right after the name.
- Minimize keeps the conversation; close ends the widget. Both are 24px round buttons on `color-control`.
- Always 52px tall (`size-header`) on `color-surface-header`. Never put other actions here.
