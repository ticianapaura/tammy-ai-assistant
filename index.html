Tammy · AI Assistant · White Label

An AI assistant chat that hands the conversation over to a human agent when it needs to, built so any merchant can rebrand it by changing a name, an icon and four colors. I designed the flows, turned them into a design system and coded the components behind it.

components

  • AssistantMessageAn answer from Tammy or from a live agent: the assistant mark, the text, and thumbs to rate it.
  • BannerA message about the conversation itself: an error with a way out, or the security notice.
  • ButtonThe few buttons outside the Composer.
  • ChatHeaderThe top bar of the chat window: the assistant's name with its mark, and the minimize and close buttons.
  • ComposerWhere the person writes. Grows with the text, counts characters, and blocks sending past the limit.
  • FeedbackTextareaThe free-text box inside the satisfaction survey.
  • OptionButtonsNumbered quick replies under an answer that lists options.
  • RatingScaleThe 1–5 satisfaction scale, from "Very unsatisfied" to "Very satisfied".
  • SatisfactionSurveyThe card shown after a live chat session ends.
  • StatusPillMarks a change in who the person is talking to.
  • SystemNoteA centered note from the system, not from a person: queue status, ticket updates, a new conversation starting.
  • TypingIndicator"Writing..." while Tammy prepares an answer.
  • UserMessageWhat the person wrote, right-aligned in a bordered bubble.
  • WelcomeThe empty state before the first message: greeting plus the security notice.
